The HDF Glow module (`HDF.GRM`) adds the glow of a highlight diffusion filter to each recorded JPEG, at 1/8, 1/4, or 1/2, with an optional CineStill halation around lights, a Black Mist veil that lifts the shadows (Light or Heavy), and an anamorphic Streak from point lights (Blue or Amber).
