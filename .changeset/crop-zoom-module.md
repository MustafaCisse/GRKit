The Crop Zoom module (`CROPZOOM.GRM`) gives a 35, 40, or 50 mm field of view at the full JPEG size: each recorded JPEG is the centre of the frame resampled to its whole size with a Catmull-Rom filter, the DNG keeps the full sensor, and the live view shows the frame. Half Frame 3:4 sets the 4:3 aspect, so the camera held upright records a portrait picture in the shape of a half-frame negative.
